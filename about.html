Bringing clarity to shared spaces.

Vyshnavi's LabTrack is a College Lab Equipment Management System designed to organise laboratory resources in one place. It helps administrators maintain equipment details, manage student and faculty records, record issue and return transactions, and identify damaged or unavailable items.

The project uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript, Node.js and Express, and MySQL with three connected tables.
